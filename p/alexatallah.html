2018 年与人联合创办 OpenSea 并任 CTO，走完 NFT 狂潮，2022 年离开。2023 年创办 OpenRouter：在所有模型厂商前面架一个统一 API，带路由、降级和按量计费——让产品换模型不用换代码，也因此成为观察「开发者真正在跑哪些模型」最清楚的公开窗口之一。2025 年完成 a16z 与 Menlo 领投的 4000 万美元融资、估值 5 亿美元，2026 年 3 月再融 1.13 亿美元、估值约 13 亿美元；同年 8 月 Stripe 同意收购，交易额据报道超过 70 亿美元。斯坦福计算机专业。
Co-founded OpenSea in 2018 and was its CTO through the NFT boom, leaving in 2022. Started OpenRouter in 2023 as a single API in front of every model provider, with routing, fallbacks and usage-based billing — the layer that lets a product switch models without switching code, and one of the clearest public windows onto which models developers actually run. Raised $40M led by a16z and Menlo at a $500M valuation in 2025, then $113M at roughly $1.3B in March 2026; Stripe agreed to acquire the company in August 2026 in a deal reported above $7B. Stanford CS.
