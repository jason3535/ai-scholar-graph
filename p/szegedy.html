匈牙利裔。波恩大学博士。在 Google 创造 Inception 架构（GoogLeNet）并与 Goodfellow 开创对抗样本研究。开发 Batch Normalization。2023年联合创办 xAI，2025年2月离开。
Hungarian-born computer scientist. PhD from the University of Bonn. Joined Google Research where he created the Inception architecture (GoogLeNet), winner of ILSVRC 2014, and co-introduced the study of adversarial examples in neural networks with Ian Goodfellow. Also developed Batch Normalization, a technique now used in virtually all deep networks. Left Google to co-found xAI in 2023. Departed xAI in February 2025.
